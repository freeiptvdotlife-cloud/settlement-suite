# Settlement Suite

> Free legal settlement and compensation calculators for plaintiffs, claimants, and families navigating the legal system — no sign-up, no data stored.

**Status:** 🚧 In Development &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Domain:** [recoverynumbers.com/settlement](https://recoverynumbers.com/settlement)

---

## Why

Understanding what a personal injury settlement, workers' comp claim, or probate estate might be worth is nearly impossible without an attorney — and attorneys are expensive. Settlement Suite gives claimants a private, no-cost starting point before those conversations happen.

## Calculators

| Tool | What it answers |
|------|----------------|
| **Personal Injury Settlement Estimator** | Projected settlement range based on damages, liability, and jurisdiction |
| **Workers' Compensation Calculator** | Benefit amounts, wage replacement, and permanent disability estimates |
| **Family Law Financial Planner** | Alimony, child support, and asset division scenarios |
| **Probate Estate Estimator** | Net estate value after debts, fees, and state probate costs |
| **Debt Relief Comparator** | Negotiated settlement vs. consolidation vs. bankruptcy trade-offs |

## Tech

Built with Astro, deployed as a static site on Cloudflare Pages. All calculations run client-side.

## Disclaimer

Estimates only. Not legal or financial advice. Always consult a licensed attorney for your specific situation.
